# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: tech recruiters and engineers screening Shumokh for software engineering internships. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or a job application, skim for 30–90 seconds, and are deciding whether to shortlist her. They want proof of real shipped work, the stack she uses, and signs of ownership and leadership beyond coursework.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Shumokh Alsharif, B.Sc. Software Engineering student at the University of Jeddah (started 2023, graduating 2027). Success: a recruiter leaves knowing what she builds (web dev + UI/UX, full-stack, expanding into DevOps), has opened at least one project, and has a way to contact her.

## Positioning

A developer who cares how things feel as much as how they work ("A website should feel just as good as it functions"). Works across frontend and backend, "mostly interested in the messy middle where they meet." The site itself is the proof: its craft and motion are part of the pitch.

## Operating Context

Static single page hosted on GitHub Pages with a custom domain (CNAME). Visitors on desktop (recruiters at a laptop) and phone (opened from LinkedIn).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. GSAP + ScrollTrigger from CDN; Lenis smooth scroll.
- Must work without JS (content visible) and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Project media lightbox (videos + screenshots) and certificate lightbox are existing functions to keep.

## Brand Commitments

- Playing-card identity is binding: custom ace artwork (assets/ace-of-*.svg), "SA" mark, personal logo (assets/1st-logo-*.svg), card-themed copy ("My Deck of Work", "Deal Me In"), 221B / A113 easter eggs, the jack-of-all-trades footer quote.
- Display face Odibee Sans, body SN Pro, cream ground, deep red (#662020) footer.

## Evidence on Hand

- Projects: Medixa, Atelier, Computer Vision & Hand Tracking, SeedSync, IoT Exhibition Archive, with mockups and demo videos in assets/.
- Awards/certs: Best Project Award (Web Dev, SE Dept., UJ), Certificate of Excellence (UJ, 2025–2026), IBM Intro to DevOps (Coursera 2026), IBM Agile & Scrum (Coursera 2026).
- Leadership, clubs and hackathons: NOT yet provided. Use clearly marked placeholders; never invent roles, clubs, events or wins.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim sits next to a project, artifact or certificate.
2. Scannable in a minute, rewarding in five.
3. The craft is the pitch, but motion never hides content.
4. Honest content: placeholders stay visibly placeholders until real entries arrive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable lightboxes and interactive cards, visible focus, reduced-motion fallback, readable contrast on cream and black grounds.
