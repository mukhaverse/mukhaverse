Medixa

01 / 05Full-stack development

A full-stack medication safety platform helping patients check whether their prescriptions are safe to combine. Users can scan pill packaging using OCR to pull the medication name automatically, then run an instant interaction check for severity, clinical significance, and management guidance, sourced from the RxNorm API, with a live Socket.io chat to message a pharmacist directly.

Computer Vision & Hand Tracking

03 / 05Computer vision

Three real-time hand-tracking experiments built with Python, OpenCV, and MediaPipe:

  • Gesture-Controlled Mouse: cursor movement and clicks driven entirely by hand position and pinch gestures
  • Particle Visuals: a GPU-rendered swarm of particles pulled into flowing, looping patterns by two-handed pinch gestures
  • Hill Climb Racing Control: steering and acceleration mapped to hand-tracking input
  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • MediaPipe
  • ModernGL
